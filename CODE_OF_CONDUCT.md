# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participation must remain respectful, technical, and constructive.
Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background. Disagreement is
expected; harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence is gathered.
- Respect security embargoes, licenses, trademarks, and contributor rights.
- Remove sensitive or customer information from examples and logs.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to fork under
  the project's open-source license.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the project owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/01o00o10). Do not include sensitive details
in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close threads,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, impact,
and the safety of affected participants. Reports will be handled as privately
as reasonably possible.
